# Brand

**CXFOS Ventures Inc.** is a holding company. It has no public-facing brand of
its own, and nothing here is customer-facing.

Each product owns its brand, and the guidelines live with the product:

| Product | Brand guidance |
| --- | --- |
| PlastyLab | `cxforge/plastylab`, `docs/design/brand.md` |

## For AI assistants

- The corporation is **CXFOS Ventures Inc.** Do not use the organisation slug
  `cxforge` as a company name in prose, documentation or customer-facing copy.
- Products are named independently and are never prefixed with the company
  name.
- Brand strategy, positioning and naming history are **not public**. They live
  in the private venture context repository.
